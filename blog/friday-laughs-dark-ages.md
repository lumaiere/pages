I was looking for a cheerful topic this week. Somehow I ended up reading about the Dark Ages. After three hours I concluded that medieval optimism was basically, "Well... at least the wolves only took one neighbor."

## Looking Back

It is easy to laugh at the people of the Dark Ages, but they survived wars, disease, famine, brutal winters, and taxes.

Actually... maybe the taxes part hasn't changed all that much.

They built cathedrals that still stand, preserved knowledge that could easily have vanished, and somehow managed to keep civilization moving forward while living in conditions that would make modern camping feel luxurious.

So here's to them.

May we never again confuse "authentic medieval experience" with "good vacation idea."
