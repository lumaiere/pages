# TikTok Repost: The Button That Might Save Your Feed (Or Haunt It Forever)

Ever scrolled TikTok, spotted a video that hits different, and hovered over that little Repost arrow wondering if it’s a magic wand or a self-destruct button? Same. Let’s clear the air with zero judgment and maximum giggles—because life’s too short for algorithm anxiety.

So what does reposting even mean? It’s TikTok’s classy way of saying “This slaps, pass it on.” Tap Repost and the video slides into your followers’ For You feeds with your name attached like a friendly “I approve this message.” It’s not a regular share that vanishes into the void—it actually lives on your profile too.

Am I reposting other people’s videos or my own? Almost always other creators’. It’s the ultimate digital high-five. Reposting your own? You can, but it feels like clapping for yourself at a comedy show. The original video keeps its stats untouched while the repost gets its own fresh adventure.

What happens if you repost your 990-view, 80-like banger? The original stays exactly as-is. The repost version racks up its own views and likes on your profile (hello, dedicated Reposts tab). Think of it as giving your video a cute little clone with better networking skills.

What good does it do? Plenty. It tells the algorithm you and your crew actually like this content, which can snowball visibility for the original creator. It’s free, wholesome promotion—and the kind of signal the For You gods reward.

As for when your account will recover from that regrettable cat-video phase… the algorithm is petty but not eternal. Start reposting solid non-feline gems, keep posting consistently, and it slowly forgives you. Might take a couple weeks. Or it might decide cats are your brand now. Either way, own it.

Fun tidbit: reposts don’t count against your daily upload limit, so you can spread joy without getting throttled. You can also undo any repost faster than you can say “regret.”

Ready to test the waters? Hop onto TikTok at https://www.tiktok.com/ and give that button some love. For the official how-to and rules, swing by TikTok’s support page on exploring videos and reposting.
