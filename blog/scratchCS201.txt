Course framing:

You have already covered programming fundamentals, algorithms, data structures, debugging, modularity, testing, and the first layers of machine behavior. That means the next step is understanding what kind of machinery is actually running your code, how layers fit together, and why the machine keeps making your abstractions pay rent.

Course map:

Episode 1 - How a Computer Actually Runs a Program - https://medium.com/@DaveLumAI/how-a-computer-actually-runs-a-program-or-why-your-code-does-not-levitate-into-reality-by-positive-2c0127117462 
From source code to running process, with fewer magical assumptions

Focus:

* source code, compilation, and execution
* instructions, processes, and runtime
* what the operating system is doing for you
* why "the computer" is really several layers pretending to be one thing

Why here:
Because students are ready to stop treating execution like sorcery.

Episode 2 - Bits, Bytes, and Data Representation - https://medium.com/@DaveLumAI/episode-2-bits-bytes-and-data-representation-or-how-everything-becomes-zeros-ones-and-88e98577be19
How everything becomes zeros, ones, and increasingly specific lies

Focus:

* binary and hexadecimal
* integer and floating-point representation
* text encoding
* why representation choices matter

Why here:
Because software manipulates symbols, but machines manipulate bits.

Episode 3 - Logic, Gates, and Digital Circuits - https://medium.com/@DaveLumAI/episode-3-logic-gates-and-digital-circuits-or-tiny-electrical-decisions-all-the-way-down-683a8043c6fc
Tiny electrical decisions all the way down

Focus:

* boolean logic in hardware
* gates and combinational logic
* stateful circuits and memory elements
* how hardware implements simple reasoning

Why here:
Because computer science gets much funnier once you realize AND and OR became physical objects.

Episode 4 - Assembly Language and the Machine Model - https://medium.com/@DaveLumAI/episode-4-assembly-language-and-the-machine-model-or-programming-with-fewer-comforts-and-more-231b34dae026
Programming with fewer comforts and more consequences

Focus:

* registers, memory, and instructions
* load, store, jump, compare
* the relationship between high-level code and low-level execution
* why assembly is educational even when you never want to live there

Why here:
Because seeing the lower layer sharpens understanding of everything above it.

Episode 5 - CPU Architecture and Performance Basics - https://medium.com/@DaveLumAI/episode-5-cpu-architecture-and-performance-basics-or-why-your-processor-is-fast-weird-and-4ddf794b13d2 
Why processors are fast, weird, and occasionally misunderstood on purpose

Focus:

* instruction cycles
* pipelining and caching
* memory hierarchy
* what actually affects performance

Why here:
Because students need a first systems-level performance model.

Episode 6 - Processes, Threads, and Concurrency Basics - https://medium.com/@DaveLumAI/episode-6-processes-threads-and-concurrency-basics-or-how-one-machine-does-many-things-at-once-6ef3836f4ebf
How one machine does many things at once and somehow blames you when it goes wrong

Focus:

* processes vs threads
* scheduling
* shared memory
* race conditions in beginner terms

Why here:
Because modern programs do not politely take turns.

Episode 7 - Operating Systems in Plain English
The adult in the room that keeps programs from elbowing each other to death

Focus:

* process management
* memory management
* file systems
* permissions and isolation

Why here:
Because once students know programs run, they should know what keeps them running.
