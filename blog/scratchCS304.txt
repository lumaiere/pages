Course:
CS304: Networks and Distributed Systems Foundations

Course framing:

Eventually one machine is not enough, one process is not enough, and one failure becomes everybody's problem.

Course map:

Episode 1 - How Computers Talk Over Networks
A polite version of shouting packets into the void

Focus:

* basic networking concepts
* addresses, routing, and protocols
* client-server thinking
* what the internet is actually doing

Why here:
Because connected software needs connected mental models.

Episode 2 - Layers and Protocols
Because one giant protocol for everything would be a nightmare in better branding

Focus:

* layered communication
* responsibilities of different protocol levels
* abstraction in networking
* why layering helps humans reason about complexity

Why here:
Because networking is organized through boundaries, just like software.

Episode 3 - Reliability Over Unreliable Systems
How to build trust on top of dropped messages and bad timing

Focus:

* retries
* ordering
* timeouts
* idempotency in plain English

Why here:
Because distributed systems begin where certainty ends.

Episode 4 - Distributed State and Coordination
The moment several machines need to agree about anything at all

Focus:

* shared state problems
* coordination cost
* replication
* why consensus is famous for ruining everyone's afternoon

Why here:
Because scale introduces agreement problems, not just more copies.

Episode 5 - Fault Tolerance and Resilience
Designing systems that bend instead of collapse theatrically

Focus:

* redundancy
* failure domains
* graceful degradation
* designing for partial failure

Why here:
Because distributed systems fail a little at a time, not all at once.

Episode 6 - Cloud and Service Architecture Basics
Computers in other buildings, billed monthly

Focus:

* services and infrastructure
* elasticity and deployment
* APIs between services
* the practical shape of modern systems

Why here:
Because this is where networking and software architecture meet real-world deployment.
