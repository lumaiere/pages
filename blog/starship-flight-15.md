## Wait, Have Humans Flown in Starship Yet?

**Nope! Not a single astronaut has flown aboard Starship.**

NASA plans to test docking with a Starship test vehicle during Artemis III in 2027, but the astronauts won't enter Starship during that demonstration.

[NASA is targeting 2028 for its next crewed lunar landing](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/artemis/nasas-artemis-iii-mission-to-test-future-moon-lander-operations/), although the specific lunar lander hasn't been selected.
