Course:
CS302: Programming Languages and Compiler Basics

Course framing:

Students have used languages. Now they get to understand why languages are shaped the way they are, and how code becomes executable machinery without a wizard hiding backstage.

Course map:

Episode 1 - What a Programming Language Is - https://medium.com/@DaveLumAI/cs302-episode-1-what-a-programming-language-is-or-more-than-syntax-less-than-religion-though-2be89ef1beb0?sharedUserId=DaveLumAI
More than syntax, less than religion, though some people blur that line

Focus:

* syntax vs semantics
* language design choices
* types, expressions, and control flow
* why languages feel different even when solving the same task

Why here:
Because languages are design artifacts, not just tools.

Episode 2 - Parsing and Structure - https://medium.com/@DaveLumAI/cs302-episode-2-parsing-and-structure-or-how-your-compiler-turns-a-wall-of-text-into-something-it-ebc5df871034?sharedUserId=DaveLumAI
How text becomes something a compiler can reason about

Focus:

* tokens and grammars
* parse trees
* syntax errors
* structured meaning from raw text

Why here:
Because compilers need to understand form before meaning.

Episode 3 - Type Systems and Meaning - https://medium.com/@DaveLumAI/cs302-episode-3-type-systems-and-meaning-or-how-languages-try-to-stop-you-before-runtime-has-to-02fda0779c47?sharedUserId=DaveLumAI
How languages try to stop you before runtime has to

Focus:

* static vs dynamic typing
* type safety
* inference
* what types are really buying you

Why here:
Because the idea of correctness begins earlier than execution.

Episode 4 - Interpretation vs Compilation - https://medium.com/@DaveLumAI/cs302-episode-4-interpretation-vs-compilation-or-two-ways-to-turn-your-intentions-into-605a35aa1f52?sharedUserId=DaveLumAI
Two ways to turn your intentions into consequences

Focus:

* interpreted and compiled models
* runtime behavior
* portability and performance
* what these labels do and do not mean

Why here:
Because students should stop using these words as mood descriptions.

Episode 5 - Compiler Pipeline Basics - https://medium.com/@DaveLumAI/cs302-episode-5-compiler-pipeline-basics-or-how-your-code-gets-taken-apart-judged-rearranged-6a79a042b755?sharedUserId=DaveLumAI
How your code gets transformed step by step into something the machine can obey

Focus:

* lexing
* parsing
* semantic analysis
* optimization and code generation

Why here:
Because this is one of the best capstone views of the programming stack.

Episode 6 - Language Design Tradeoffs
No language is perfect. They are all just making different promises

Focus:

* safety vs flexibility
* simplicity vs power
* expressiveness vs predictability
* why style wars never truly end

Why here:
Because students should leave with judgment, not just vocabulary.
