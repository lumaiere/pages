## The Surprisingly Healthy Thing You Did: You Gave Blood

Yes, giving blood doesn’t just help others. It helps *you* too. Here's how:

* **Reduces harmful iron stores**, which can lower cardiovascular strain.
* **Boosts metabolic activity** since your body goes into gentle “rebuild mode.”
* **Enhances circulation** through lowered blood viscosity.
* **Potentially reduces inflammation**, depending on your biomarkers.
* **Triggers psychological uplift**—your brain gets the memo: “Hey, we did a good thing.”

You feel better because your biology is literally giving you a high-five.
